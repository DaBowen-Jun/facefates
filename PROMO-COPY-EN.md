## Usage Notes

- Always append the entertainment disclaimer on paid channels (Meta/Google are strict about fortune-telling claims).
- Avoid words like "predict", "guarantee", "accurate fortune" in **ad copy** — use "report", "reading", "map", "insight" instead.
- On Reddit/Product Hunt, lead with the **privacy angle** (photo never uploaded) — it's the strongest differentiator and the most upvoted hook.
- On Instagram/TikTok, lead with the **"weirdly specific"** hook — it drives the highest comment engagement.
